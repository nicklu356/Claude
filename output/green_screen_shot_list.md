# HearHere video: green-screen shot list

- **Green:** pure chroma green `#00B140`. Key it out in CapCut, Premiere or DaVinci (Ultra Key / Chroma Key), then put a real photo on the track underneath.
- **Subtitles:** none are burned in. `potato_video.srt` is included in case you want them later.
- **Shadows:** the characters have no shadows, so they key cleanly. After keying, add a soft dark ellipse under each person and the post so they look grounded.
- **Ground line:** in the street shots, Jason's feet and the post's base sit at about **y = 960 px** (counting down from the top of the frame). Line up the pavement in your photo with that height.
- **Green-ish UI colour:** the UI's "success / OK" colour is now teal-blue (instead of green) so it isn't keyed out with the background.
